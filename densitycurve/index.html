The density as a limit of histograms

With few samples a histogram is ragged. As the sample grows it settles onto a smooth curve: the probability density of the population.

Try this

  1. Start with a handful of samples. Can you guess the shape of the population?
  2. Turn on the density curve. How many samples until the histogram follows it closely?

Bar heights are scaled so the total area is 1, like a density.
