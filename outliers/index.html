Regression outliers and leverage

Drag the orange point around and watch how much it pulls on the regression line, and how the outlier diagnostics respond.

Leverage measures how unusual a point's x-value is. Points far from the mean of x can pull the line strongly.

Cook's distance measures how much the fitted line changes when the point is left out. It combines leverage and the size of the residual.

DFBETAS measures how much the slope changes, in standard errors, when the point is left out.

Dotted lines mark common rule-of-thumb thresholds.

Try this

  1. Find a position with high leverage but a small Cook's distance.
  2. Where does the point have the most influence on the slope?
