Normal approximation to the binomial

The bars show the binomial distribution \(B(n, p)\). The curve is the normal distribution with the same mean \(\mu = np\) and variance \(\sigma^2 = np(1-p)\).

A common rule of thumb: the approximation works well as long as \(np(1-p) > 5\).
