Step 3 · what to watch for

The real experiment found T = 8. Shuffle a few times: how often does pure chance alone hand you 8 or more? Once the whole class has pooled its draws, that proportion is an estimate of P(T ≥ 8) — the answer to “could this result be due to chance?”
